description: Write or revise concise public and internal prose without changing protected wording.
---

# Terse writing

- Use short sentences, direct verbs, and concrete labels.
- Remove throat-clearing, repetition, and vague qualifiers.
- Write in the present tense. No "we used to", "previously", "no longer", "formerly", or incident backstory; git holds
  the past.
- No em dashes in new or revised prose; use periods, commas, colons, or parentheses.
- Keep the meaning, audience, and authority of the source intact.
- Ask before changing protected wording: quotations, legal text, workshop scripts, and any wording you are not
  authorized to rewrite.
