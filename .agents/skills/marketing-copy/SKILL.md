# Marketing copy

Edit approved public copy in the repository. Save private marketing plans and campaign strategy in Notion through
[notion-marketing-plans](../notion-marketing-plans/SKILL.md) only when the user asks to retain a plan.

Read [`docs/marketing-copy.md`](../../../docs/marketing-copy.md), the page's file under `neon/locales/en/`,
[`docs/agent-decision-councils.md`](../../../docs/agent-decision-councils.md), and
[`docs/public-contributor-safety.md`](../../../docs/public-contributor-safety.md). Edit firm marketing copy in those
YAML files, not in the Rust loaders.

- Lead with the customer's needs, wishes, decisions, and next step. Address the reader as "you" and describe how the
  service helps them. Keep firm identification in the shared footer; avoid repeating the operating firm or responsible
  attorney in marketing paragraphs unless a specific required disclosure belongs there.
- Make plain, specific claims about the service. Avoid comparisons, superlatives, guarantees, and unsupported
  outcome or certification claims.
- Verify every concrete fact against its canonical repository source or with the user; do not infer it from nearby
  copy.
- Convene the Legal Council for legal-copy decisions and the Client Council for visitor-facing decisions.
- Do not expose client facts, legal files, real personal contact details, or production identifiers in public copy.
