# Appear and file in a Douglas County, Nevada misdemeanor DUI

Sources, checked 2026-11-11: [East Fork Justice Court](https://eastforkjusticecourt.com/) and [Tahoe Justice
Court](https://tahoejusticecourt.com/) (both reached from douglascountynv.gov's court links), their contact, FAQ,
schedule, fee, and form pages; the [District Attorney](https://dcnvda.org/ContactUs.shtml) (reached from
douglascountynv.gov); the [District Court e-filing page](https://douglasdistrictcourt.com/e-filing/); [NRS
169](https://www.leg.state.nv.us/NRS/NRS-169.html), [174](https://www.leg.state.nv.us/NRS/NRS-174.html),
[189](https://www.leg.state.nv.us/NRS/NRS-189.html), [484C](https://www.leg.state.nv.us/NRS/NRS-484C.html); and DMV's
[hearings page](https://dmv.nv.gov/admlaw.htm) and [brochure](https://dmv.nv.gov/pdfforms/hearingsbrochure.pdf). Neither
justice court publishes local rules or an attorney filing guide online. If the clerk says otherwise, follow the clerk
and update this skill.

## Hard rules

- The agent never signs in anywhere, never types a password, code, or payment detail, and never solves a CAPTCHA.
- The agent never clicks **Submit**, **Pay**, **Send**, or a fax-send control, and never mails anything. It prepares,
  shows the lawyer, and stops. Email goes out only as a Gmail draft the lawyer sends.
- No client name, Project code, or matter fact goes into this skill.

## 1. Which court, and how it takes paper

- **East Fork**: East Fork Township, **outside the Lake Tahoe basin**: Carson Valley (Minden, Gardnerville) and Topaz
  Lake. 1038 Buckeye Rd., P.O. Box 218, Minden, NV 89423; telephone and fax on the [court's
  site](https://eastforkjusticecourt.com/). Second floor of the Judicial and Law Enforcement Building ([DA
  FAQ](https://dcnvda.org/CRD_FAQs.shtml)).
- **Tahoe**: The Lake Tahoe area, from rural Carson City on Hwy 28 to the Stateline casinos and Daggett Summit on
  Kingsbury Grade. 175 Hwy. 50, P.O. Box 7169, Stateline, NV 89449; telephone and fax on its [contact
  page](https://tahoejusticecourt.com/contact/).
- **From the citation**: The court is printed on the citation and the appearance date "is on the bottom"
  ([citations](https://eastforkjusticecourt.com/citations/)). The court says to inspect it carefully, because people pay
  the wrong court. A citation for a misdemeanor "serves as an official complaint" ([criminal
  charges](https://eastforkjusticecourt.com/criminal-charges/)). Otherwise, map where the stop happened to the boundary
  above.
- **Case number**: **Unconfirmed.** Neither court publishes its format. Read it off the citation, notice of setting, or
  the clerk.
- **E-filing**: **None found for either justice court.** The county's portal, `https://efile.douglasdistrictcourt.com/`,
  is the Ninth Judicial District Court's ("District Court records and filing services"); its support address is on the
  portal. Ask the clerk before assuming otherwise.
- **Paper**: At the clerk's counter or by mail to the P.O. box. Tahoe has **no drop box**
  ([FAQ](https://tahoejusticecourt.com/faq/)).
- **Fax**: East Fork takes a remote-appearance request by counter, fax, or mail
  ([appearing](https://eastforkjusticecourt.com/appearing-in-court/)). Whether either court accepts **other** filings by
  fax or email is **unconfirmed**: call the clerk. Both FAQs say an attorney may not request adjudication of a citation
  by fax or email.
- **District Court**: Appeals from a justice-court judgment and felonies (a third DUI in seven years is a felony, [DUI
  page](https://eastforkjusticecourt.com/dui/)). It e-files through the portal above; a first appearance there means
  emailing a notice of appearance to the Clerk's Office. Not covered further here.

## 2. What must be on paper

- **Notice of appearance.** Neither court posts a form or a rule for one; draft it on the court's caption. The
  courts' own form caption reads `IN THE EAST FORK JUSTICE COURT` / `COUNTY OF DOUGLAS, STATE OF NEVADA`, with `CASE
  NO.:` top left ([form](https://eastforkjusticecourt.com/forms/)). Use the same pattern for Tahoe.
- **Discovery request** to the District Attorney under NRS 174.235 (Title 14 governs criminal procedure "in the courts
  of the State of Nevada", NRS 169.025). Ask for the defendant's statements, witness statements, test results and
  reports, and the State's case-in-chief documents (NRS 174.235(1)). Exculpatory evidence is owed regardless (subs. 3).
- **Continuance.** By written stipulation with the District Attorney, received before the appearance, or by a timely
  written motion ([EF FAQ](https://eastforkjusticecourt.com/faq/), [Tahoe FAQ](https://tahoejusticecourt.com/faq/)). A
  mandatory appearance date moves only that way ([citations](https://eastforkjusticecourt.com/citations/)).
- **Dismissal before arraignment** needs the District Attorney's written consent (both FAQs).
- **Remote appearance.** Use the court's "Request to Appear by Phone or Video" (East Fork, rev. Feb. 2026, SCR Part
  IX) or "Request to Appear Remotely" (Tahoe). In person is required until an approval order issues.

## 3. Check the document

- Final: no brackets, placeholders, or draft marks; signed by the lawyer with bar number and the Firm's block.
- Format (paper size, line numbering, margins): **unconfirmed** for these courts. No local rule is online; ask the
  clerk, and use letter size with the caption above until told otherwise.
- **Remote-appearance timing conflicts.** The East Fork web page says 48 hours before the hearing; its February 2026
  form says filed and served **14 calendar days** before in criminal cases, with exhibits to the other party and the
  court by noon the business day before. Meet the 14 days; explain on the form if late.
- **Discovery timing (NRS 174.285):** request within **30 days after arraignment** (later only with leave); the State
  complies not less than 30 days before trial. Continuing duty and sanctions: NRS 174.295.
- **Fees** ([East Fork fee schedule](https://eastforkjusticecourt.com/download/justice-court-fees/), "Version 2"): it
  lists no fee for a criminal appearance, motion, or discovery request. Copies $0.50/page; certification $3.00; record
  search $1.00/year; bail or property bond filing $50.00; appeal to District Court $142.00. Tahoe's schedule was not
  read: **unconfirmed**. East Fork's online payments go to Point and Pay (3%, $2 minimum), East Fork only.

## 4. Drive the submission, attorney in the loop

1. Confirm the court from the citation (§1). Save the final PDF in the matter's Navigator folder and check its sha256.
2. Prepare the route the clerk confirmed: a printed packet with an original for the court and a conformed copy back
   (with return envelope), or, for a remote-appearance request to East Fork, the fax cover and PDF.
3. If emailing the District Attorney, create a **Gmail draft** only (the `email-draft` skill). Never send it.
4. **Stop and show the lawyer** the court, case number, document, page count, and route. The lawyer files, faxes,
   mails, or sends personally. Any online payment page is the lawyer's alone.

- Lake Tahoe office: 175 Highway 50, Stateline, NV 89449.

## 7. Timing

- East Fork: Monday to Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Tahoe: 9:00 a.m. to noon and 1:00 to 5:00 p.m., closed noon to
  1:00 ([contact](https://tahoejusticecourt.com/contact/); its home page also shows 8:00 start, so confirm).
- Closed on NRS 236.015 holidays ([2026 list](https://eastforkjusticecourt.com/holiday-schedule/), including Nevada
  Day Oct. 30 and Family Day Nov. 27). No 2027 list is posted yet.
- East Fork traffic court is 1:00 p.m. Tuesdays; criminal sessions run "throughout the week"; trial "within 60 days
  unless time is waived" ([schedule](https://eastforkjusticecourt.com/court-schedule/)). Tahoe posts no schedule.
- **Appeal:** within **10 days** of the justice court's judgment (NRS 189.010).

## 8. Read the docket and record court dates

- **No online case search** for either court. Case details come "at our counter or by telephone" (both FAQs), or by a
  written Records/Document Request ([forms](https://eastforkjusticecourt.com/forms/)). Call early: Tahoe warns of
  hundreds of calls. The date of record is the citation, summons, or notice of setting.
- The DA's site links "Court Dates" to the District Court calendar, `https://douglasdistrictcourt.com/calendar/`,
  which is **unconfirmed** to include justice-court settings.

```bash
navigator project deadline create --project <code> --host <host> --title "<what is due>" \
  --due-at <RFC 3339 with offset> --authority-kind court_order --authority "<notice of setting and date>" \
  --calendar-role firm --category court --source lawyer --ics notes/<YYYY.MM.DD>_<slug>.ics
```

Hearings (arraignment, trial, remote-appearance-approved settings) take `--calendar-role hearing --category hearing`.
Also record the NRS 174.285 30-day discovery window from the arraignment date.

## 9. The separate DMV revocation clock

An implied-consent revocation is DMV's, independent of the court case ([DMV DUI](https://dmv.nv.gov/dui.htm)).

- The officer serves the order of revocation, seizes the license, and on request issues a temporary license
  **effective for only 7 days including the date of issuance** (NRS 484C.220(1)).
- The driver may request a DMV hearing in writing "at any time while" ineligible to drive, and gets one hearing; DMV
  then issues a further temporary license for the review (NRS 484C.230(1)). So the statute sets no filing deadline, but
  the practical one is **before the 7-day temporary license lapses**. For file numbers beginning `IP`, `ID`, or `RT`, no
  hearing after the revocation period ends or after reinstatement with an interlock
  ([DMV](https://dmv.nv.gov/admlaw.htm)).
- Scope: refusal, or 0.08 or a detectable prohibited substance at the time of the test (NRS 484C.230(2)). No fee.
- Request to DMV Office of Administrative Hearings, 555 Wright Way, Carson City, NV 89711-0960; fax and telephone in
  the [brochure](https://dmv.nv.gov/pdfforms/hearingsbrochure.pdf), rev. Aug. 2021). An email request needs full name,
  license number, DMV file number, mailing address, and phone; the address is obfuscated on DMV's page, so read it in
  the browser.
- Record the 7-day date as a Deadline (`--authority-kind statute --authority "NRS 484C.220(1)"`), and the hearing,
  once noticed by mail, with `--calendar-role hearing --category hearing`. Judicial review of an affirmed order is in
  district court under NRS 233B (NRS 484C.230(4)).
