  Files for the parent or student in a special education due process case at the California Office of Administrative
  Hearings (OAH), Special Education Division: the due process complaint (request for due process hearing, with or
  without mediation), a request for mediation only, motions, notices of representation, prehearing conference
  statements, and witness and exhibit lists. Covers serving the district before filing, the required contents of a
  complaint, the Secure e-File Transfer (SFT) upload driven attorney-in-the-loop, OAH's 5:00 p.m. cutoff, the IDEA

  recording OAH's dates as Navigator Deadlines. Use whenever a California special education due process document is
  ready to file, an OAH scheduling order or notice arrives, or the lawyer asks "where do I file this" in an IEP dispute
  against a California school district.

# File in a California OAH special education due process case

Sources, checked 2026-11-11: OAH's Special Education Handbook 2026 (cited as HB p. N), the File or Upload OAH Case
Documents page, Request Due Process Hearing, the [Forms][forms] page, the optional complaint form DGS OAH 59, rev.
9/2024, and eCFR 34 C.F.R. Part 300. leginfo.legislature.ca.gov refused automated reads (HTTP 403), so Education Code
sections are cited as OAH and the eCFR cite them; read the section on leginfo before quoting it. If a screen or rule
differs, follow OAH and update this skill.

**Student records are confidential.** The complaint "must remain confidential" ([34 C.F.R. 300.508(a)(1)][508]), and
IEPs, assessments, and other education records are protected by FERPA ([34 C.F.R. Part 99][ferpa]) and [Ed. Code
49076](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=EDC&sectionNum=49076) (text
unconfirmed here). Never paste a student's or parent's name, a record, or a matter fact into a prompt, issue, commit,
PR, Slack outside the matter channel, or any third-party tool.

## 1. Settle where it goes

- **Forum**: OAH, Special Education Division, 2349 Gateway Oaks Drive, Suite 200, Sacramento, CA 95833; call the number
  in the handbook and ask for a case manager (HB p. 42, 44).
- **Case number**: Ten digits, e.g. `2025050384` on a [published decision][dec]. That the first six are the filing year
  and month is unconfirmed; copy the number from OAH's first notice, never derive it.
- **Method**: **SFT**, `https://www.applications.dgs.ca.gov/oah/oahsftweb`, or U.S. mail, overnight delivery, or hand
  delivery. **OAH no longer accepts email or fax** (SFT page; HB p. 42).
- **Account**: The signing lawyer's own SFT account, tied to one email address (SFT page). The agent never registers,
  signs in, or handles the password.
- **E-service consent**: Filing a complaint through SFT agrees that OAH serves the filer through SFT until told
  otherwise (form OAH 59 p. 3).
- **Fee**: None stated on request page or in the handbook; "no filing fee" is unconfirmed. Call OAH before telling a
  client.

Case types (HB p. 28-36): **Mediation and Hearing** (most common), **Hearing Only**, **Mediation Only**, and
**Expedited** or **Dual** (discipline issues). Optional forms for each are on the [Forms][forms] page. **Mediation
Only:** an attorney may not accompany the parent at the mediation ([Ed. Code 56500.3(a)][ed], per HB p. 54), and the
case closes after it; a hearing then needs a new complaint (HB p. 28-29). Ask the lawyer before choosing it.

## 2. Serve first, on whom

- Every document is **served on every other party before it is filed**; OAH rejects a filing without a completed
  proof of service (HB p. 39-40; SFT page).
- The complaint goes to each public agency named: the district of residence, a charter school, a county office, or
  another agency. Name agencies, never individual employees (form OAH 59 p. 7). The filer sends it to the other party
  and to OAH ([34 C.F.R. 300.508(a)][508]; Ed. Code 56502(c)(1), per HB p. 159).
- A motion to add a party is also served on the new party (HB p. 139).

## 3. Check the document before filing

**A complaint** must state ([34 C.F.R. 300.508(b)][508]; HB p. 159; Ed. Code 56502(b), subdivision unconfirmed): the
child's name; residence address (contact information if homeless); school attended; the nature of the problem, **with
facts** (dates, IEP provisions; "denied FAPE for 2022-2023" is not enough, form OAH 59 p. 9); and a proposed resolution
to the extent known. Add any interpreter language for each parent, every participant's email for video, and the
student's age or birthdate (HB p. 44-45, 136; form OAH 59 p. 6-8).

- **Sufficiency:** deemed sufficient unless the district notifies OAH and the Firm in writing within **15 days of
  receipt**; the ALJ rules on the face of the complaint within **5 days** ([300.508(d)][508]; HB p. 136). If found
  insufficient, the ALJ usually allows **14 days** to refile (HB p. 136).
- **Amendment** only with written consent plus a resolution chance, or ALJ leave no later than 5 days before
  hearing; an amended complaint restarts the resolution timelines ([300.508(d)(3)-(4)][508]; HB p. 138).
- **A motion** (continuance, stay put, dismissal, amend, peremptory challenge) states the request, reasons, and
  facts; a factual one ends "declares these facts under penalty of perjury," signed and dated (HB p. 135). **Stay put**
  attaches the last agreed-upon IEP (HB p. 138).
- **Notice of representation:** the attorney files one with OAH and serves all parties; the district must have 10
  days' notice before hearing (HB p. 79; Ed. Code 56507(a) per HB p. 157). OAH publishes no form for it ([forms]).
- **Prehearing conference statement** (with witness list and exhibit list): time estimate; issues and proposed
  resolutions; each witness with a testimony summary and issue; each expert's name, address, opinion summary, and issue;
  documentary and demonstrative evidence; subpoenas issued; interpreter or accommodation needs (HB p. 65-66).
- **Exhibits are never filed with OAH.** They are uploaded to Case Center when OAH invites the parties (HB p. 40, 74).
- **Format for SFT:** PDF with a lowercase `.pdf`; 10 MB a document, 50 MB a package; letter size, pages numbered
  in sequence; name 100 characters or fewer with no hyphens, underscores, apostrophes, `&`, `#`, `%`, `/`, or `:`.
  Upload one document at a time. Special education documents need an **original signature** (SFT page); whether an `/s/`
  signature satisfies that is unconfirmed, so ask OAH or have the lawyer sign and scan.
- Final: no brackets, placeholders, or draft marks. A proof of service (OAH's optional Statement of Service, or the
  Firm's) is the last pages of the same PDF.

## 4. Drive the upload, attorney in the loop

Use the lawyer's Chrome through Claude in Chrome, in a new tab.

1. Open the SFT URL. On the sign-in page, hand the tab to the lawyer and wait. Never type a username, password,
   code, or payment detail; never solve a CAPTCHA. Six wrong passwords lock the account for two hours (SFT page).
2. Copy the PDF into the session scratchpad, renamed to SFT's rules, and check its sha256 against the Navigator copy.
3. Fill the case and document fields the screen asks for. Field labels are unconfirmed; follow the screen.
4. **Stop and show the lawyer** a screenshot: case number (or "new case"), document title, page count, the
   proof-of-service page. The lawyer clicks **Submit / Send / Upload** personally; so is any paper filing.

## 5. Service and proof of service

SFT **does not serve anyone** (SFT page; HB p. 43). Serve by mail or in person, or by email or fax only where that party
agreed in advance (HB p. 40). OAH's Consent to Electronic Service Agreement records consent to email service ([forms];
form OAH 59 p. 3). The proof of service names each party served, the method, the address or email, and the date, and is
signed. Serve the district's counsel once counsel appears; until then, the district at its office address. Draft any
cover email as a Gmail draft only; the lawyer sends it.

## 6. After filing

- SFT shows a confirmation and emails one from a `dgs.ca.gov` address (SFT page). OAH's orders arrive in SFT,
  with an email notice to registered users (HB p. 75).
- From the matter repository, save the confirmation with `navigator mail save <gmail-url>` and file what was filed:

```bash
navigator site document upload --host <host> --project <code> --kind filing --folder "Docket Filings" \
  --slug "YYYY.MM.DD_<name>.pdf" --file <filed.pdf>
```

- Complete or move the Deadline the filing satisfied with `navigator project deadline complete`.
- Archive the confirmation thread (remove `INBOX`) only after the document is filed in Navigator.

## 7. Timing

- **Cutoff: 5:00 p.m. Pacific, Monday to Friday.** SFT inboxes are monitored 8:00 to 5:00; anything later, or on a
  weekend or holiday, counts as received the next business day (SFT page). Mailed or hand-delivered oppositions count
  when received in Sacramento (HB p. 66).
- **Limit:** two years from when the parent knew or should have known of the action ([Ed. Code 56505(l)][ed56505]
  and [34 C.F.R. 300.511(e)][511], per HB p. 155), except where the district misrepresented that it had resolved the
  problem or withheld required information ([300.511(f)][511]).
- **Days are calendar days** unless a rule says business or school days ([34 C.F.R. 300.11][d11]). The district's
  response, if it sent no prior written notice, is due in **10 days** ([300.508(e)][508]).
- **Resolution session** within **15 days** of the district receiving the complaint, unless waived in writing or
  replaced by mediation; no district attorney unless the parent brings one ([34 C.F.R. 300.510(a)][510]).
- **Resolution period: 30 days**; the 45-day clock starts when it ends, or the day after a written waiver or written
  impasse ([300.510(b)-(c)][510]). A parent who skips the session delays the timelines; a district may move to dismiss
  ([300.510(b)(3)-(4)][510]; HB p. 138).
- **Decision within 45 days** after the resolution period, extendable for good cause on a party's request
  ([34 C.F.R. 300.515(a), (c)][515]; Ed. Code 56502(f), 56505(f)(3), per HB p. 76).
- **Expedited** (discipline): hearing within **20 school days** of filing, decision within **10 school days** after
  it, not continued for convenience (HB p. 36).
- **Stay put:** the child stays in the current placement during proceedings unless the parties agree otherwise
  ([34 C.F.R. 300.518][518]; Ed. Code 56505(d), per HB p. 160).
- A mediation date must be at least **35 days** after the complaint was filed; ask **10 business days** ahead (HB p.
  125) . A **request to set mediation**, every **prehearing motion**, and the **PHC statement** are due **3 business
  days before the PHC**; an opposition within **3 business days of service** (HB p. 33, 66). The **Participant
  Information** form is filed 2 business days before the PHC and not served (HB p. 62).
- **Exhibits** in Case Center by **5:00 p.m., 5 business days before hearing**; uploads close 3 business days before
  (HB p. 109; Ed. Code 56505(e)(7), per HB p. 107). Issues and proposed resolutions disclosed 10 days before hearing
  (Ed. Code 56505(e)(6), per HB p. 157).
- **Peremptory challenge** (one per party): before or at the start of the PHC, or 2 business days before hearing if
  a different ALJ hears it (HB p. 141). **Appeal** to state or federal court within **90 days** (HB p. 77).

## 8. Read the case and record deadlines

- **Scheduling Order** arrives after filing: PHC date and hearing date, and in a Mediation and Hearing case the
  deadline to request mediation, not a mediation date (HB p. 31-33). A Dual case carries two sets of dates (HB p. 37).
- PHCs are by Zoom on a Monday or Friday, usually 10:00, 1:00, or 3:00, about 7 to 10 days before hearing (HB p. 68).
  The **Order Following Prehearing Conference** sets the evidence rules; follow it exactly (HB p. 75).
- The assigned ALJ and dates are on OAH's [calendar][cal]; check it before the PHC (HB p. 65).

Record every date from an OAH order as a Navigator Deadline, citing the order:

```bash
navigator project deadline create --project <code> --host <host> --title "<what is due>" \
  --due-at <RFC 3339 with offset> --authority-kind court_order --authority "<OAH order and date>" \
  --calendar-role firm --category court --source lawyer --ics notes/<YYYY.MM.DD>_<slug>.ics
```

The PHC and hearing take `--calendar-role hearing --category hearing`. A statutory date (resolution session, 30-day
period, 45-day decision, two-year limit) takes `--authority-kind statute --authority "34 C.F.R. 300.510(a)"` or the Ed.
Code section that sets it. Compute it from the date the district **received** the complaint, and say so.
