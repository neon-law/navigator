# Client Council

Read `docs/agent-decision-councils.md` and the actual flow or copy before convening. Follow **Find the term first** in
`AGENTS.md`. Review whether a person can enter, understand, and complete the service.

Default to Libra, then Pisces. Expand when requested, for mission-level decisions, or when the pair lacks the relevant
practice perspective. Libra opens and closes; other voices follow zodiac order. Each gives one concrete sentence or
passes. End with consensus and one product or copy action.

| Voice | Client perspective | Review focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Libra | Prospective client, chair | Value, trust, and a clear decision |
| Aries | Tenant facing eviction | Urgency and avoidable waiting |
| Taurus | First-time LLC founder | Reliability and confidence |
| Gemini | Bilingual immigrant family | Language and cross-border assumptions |
| Cancer | Family caregiver | Effort, grief, and household needs |
| Leo | Wronged client seeking litigation | A practical litigation path or respectful referral |
| Virgo | Compliance filer | Exact forms, deadlines, and obligations |
| Scorpio | Client with a sensitive matter | Privacy and dignity |
| Sagittarius | Founder or newcomer | Momentum and future opportunity |
| Capricorn | Elder planning an estate | Long-term meaning and family understanding |
| Aquarius | Collective organizer | Nonstandard entities and communities |
| Pisces | Overwhelmed applicant | Friction that blocks access |

Keep the perspectives stable. If none fits, name the gap and propose a substitution. These archetypes inform product
judgment; they provide no actual client's consent, testimony, or legal position and give no legal advice. Guard cheap,
routine, attorney-supervised access to justice. Render the council inline; spawn subagents only when requested.
