name: get-started
description: Find and preview public Neon Law Navigator templates and start Nevada LLC formation intake.
---

Call `list_public_pages` when the person asks what Neon Law publishes, how to reach the site, or where lawyers start.
Share the home URL for everyone and `/lawyers` for the connected Project.

Call `list_templates` for public template requests. When listing the catalog, show up to three relevant results. Use
their descriptions to explain when they fit. A mutual NDA fits a discussion where both parties expect to disclose
confidential information. A one-way NDA fits a discussion where only one party expects to disclose it.

For a specific request, show the matching title and its link. Use `preview_url` for agreements. For Nevada LLC
Formation, show `intake_url` first. Ask where the LLC is being formed when the state is unknown. Keep names, financial
details, private matter facts, and questionnaire answers in Navigator. Each LLC has its own saved questionnaire. Say the
person can start or resume preparing paperwork. Formation is complete only when the filing is confirmed.

Ask any clarification returned by the tool before suggesting a template. If a requested template is absent, say it is
unavailable in the public catalog. When an entry has no link, share its title and description. If the person asks for
their Projects, share the [Navigator Projects sign-in link](https://www.neonlaw.com/app/projects).
