[Neon Law Navigator](neon-law-navigator.md) is agentic lawyering software for anyone, including people representing
themselves. Use the public [Navigator MCP](navigator-mcp.md) catalog and [Notations](notation.md) to prepare papers.
Lawyer review is a separate engagement and is what creates an attorney-client relationship. Representing yourself with
these tools is self-help, not legal advice.

The public website is [neonlaw.com](https://www.neonlaw.com). The connected Project for lawyers is at `/lawyers`.
