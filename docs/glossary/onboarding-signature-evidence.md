---
title: "Onboarding Signature Evidence"
description: >-
  Onboarding Signature Evidence records a lawyer's verification of a manually imported signed retainer.
---

Onboarding Signature Evidence ties the lawyer's review to the owning [Firm](firm.md), [Project](project.md), and exact
[Asset](asset.md) revision. The record preserves the signing account, request ID, completion date, and reviewing lawyer.
The Project becomes [Official](official.md) when the verified [Onboarding](onboarding.md) revision is current.

The table is `onboarding_signature_evidence`.

Schema: [`onboarding_signature_evidence`](../../store/postgres/schema.sql).
