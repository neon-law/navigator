---
title: "Notation Clause"
description: "A lawyer's additional legal prose saved for one Notation."
---

A Notation Clause belongs to one [Notation](notation.md) and supplies matter-specific wording at its custom-clause
placeholder. It is distinct from the shared versioned [Clause](clause.md) library.

The table is `notation_clause`.

Schema: [`notation_clause`](../../store/postgres/schema.sql).
