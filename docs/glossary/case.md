---
title: "Case"
description: >-
  One litigation matter before one forum, within a Project.
---

The court or other forum, case number, and posture for one proceeding inside a [Project](project.md). An engagement can
contain several cases, each with its own [Docket Entries](docket-entry.md).

The table is `case`.

Schema: [`case`](../../store/src/schema/navigator.surql#L2264).
