---
title: Legal Plan Filing Rule
description: >-
  The Firm's verified filing milestone, calendar-day window, destination, and filer for a Legal Plan benefit.
---

A **Legal Plan Filing Rule** covers one exact benefit code on a [Legal Plan](legal-plan.md) from a recorded effective
date. The service date selects the applicable rule. A [Legal Plan Claim](legal-plan-claim.md) keeps its selected rule
and any recorded filing [Deadline](deadline.md).

An authorized Firm administrator imports rules from a private configuration file. [Legal plans](../legal-plans.md)
describes the import and claim workflow.

The table is `legal_plan_filing_rule`.
