---
title: "Handoff Acknowledgment"
description: "The recipient's acknowledgment of the exact manifest of one Handoff Bundle."
---

The acknowledgment names the [Handoff Bundle](handoff-bundle.md), its manifest digest, the acknowledging Person, and the
acknowledgment time. Its immutable record binds the acknowledgment to the files offered.

The table is `project_handoff_acknowledgment`.

Schema: [`project_handoff_acknowledgment`](../../store/postgres/schema.sql).
