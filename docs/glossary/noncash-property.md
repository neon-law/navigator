---
title: "Noncash Property"
description: "Noncash Property is identified property recorded by quantity or ownership interest in a Firm journal."
---

Noncash Property has its own property identity and exact quantity or ownership interest. Its custody evidence,
description, award, actual receipt, valuation, and eventual disposal remain distinct. Property is not a stored byte
[Asset](asset.md). Evidence is a private [Document](document.md).

A [Journal Posting](journal-posting.md) records the property identity and quantity in its noncash unit. Source Snapshot
facts retain the description and custody reference. An unknown reporting value remains unknown; a later valuation keeps
the original property and quantity intact. An [Estate Asset](estate-asset.md) is a separate estate-plan schedule line.
