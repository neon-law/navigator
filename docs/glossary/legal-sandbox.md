The private workspace where a [Neon Law Nautilus](neon-law-nautilus.md) subscriber works with Claude Sonnet on their own
legal questions and drafts. The subscription includes $5 of model usage each month; usage beyond that is priced à la
carte, with pricing provided by Lex Tecnica.

It is **client-English, not a schema noun**. Working in the sandbox is self-help: it does not create an attorney-client
relationship or privilege. A subscriber who wants a lawyer to check the result asks for a lawyer review, which is legal
work under its own engagement and prepaid.
