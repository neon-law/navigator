---
title: "Answer"
description: >-
  One respondent's recorded response to one Question.
---

A response to a [Question](question.md), recording the respondent, author, and source. Corrections append another
response; a Notation reads the latest response for its questionnaire state.

The table is `answer`.

Schema: [`answer`](../../store/src/schema/navigator.surql#L1572).
