---
title: "Notation Rate"
description: >-
  A catalog price for one unit of Notation work.
---

A named unit and price used when quoting [Notation](notation.md) work. A [Notation Meter Entry](notation-meter-entry.md)
preserves the rate actually quoted.

The table is `notation_rate`.

Schema: [`notation_rate`](../../store/src/schema/navigator.surql#L1784).
