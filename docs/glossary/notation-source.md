---
title: "Notation Source"
description: >-
  One input recorded as informing a Notation.
---

The provenance of an input to a [Notation](notation.md), such as a [Communication](communication.md) or questionnaire
facts. The record links the source and its ordering without copying the answer body.

The table is `notation_source`.

Schema: [`notation_source`](../../store/src/schema/navigator.surql#L1813).
