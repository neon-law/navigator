title: "Certified Mail"

---

The workflow prefix `certified_mail` records a lawyer-driven outbound certified-mail submission. It is an outbound
submission step and must sit behind [Lawyer Review](lawyer-review.md). See
[`notation-authoring`](../notation-authoring.md#what-runs-after-intake--the-step-registry) and
[`workflows::step::STEP_PREFIXES`](../../workflows/src/step.rs).
