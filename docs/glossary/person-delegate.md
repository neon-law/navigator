---
title: "Person Delegate"
description: >-
  A recorded relationship in which one client helps another with their matters.
---

The recorded basis on which a helper assists a [Person](person.md), with who recorded it and any revocation. This record
itself grants no authority to act through a session.

The table is `person_delegate`.

Schema: [`person_delegate`](../../store/src/schema/navigator.surql#L704).
