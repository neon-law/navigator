---
title: "Evidence Share Usage"
description: >-
  Evidence Share Usage records daily totals for views and downloads of shared evidence.
---

Navigator aggregates [Evidence Share](evidence-share.md) views and download requests by UTC date. Daily totals omit
recipient, invitation, and matter identifiers.

The table is `evidence_share_usage`.

Schema: [`evidence_share_usage`](../../store/postgres/schema.sql).
