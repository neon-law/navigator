title: "Statutory Deadline"
description: >-
  A dated statutory obligation recorded for one Project.

A [Project](project.md) obligation with its triggering date, due date, statutory citation, source, and status. The
general-purpose docketed obligation is a [Deadline](deadline.md).

The table is `statutory_deadline`.
