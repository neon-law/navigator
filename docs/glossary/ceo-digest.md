title: CEO Digest
description: >-
  The weekly brief a Firm's Owner receives, written from journaled queries and
  checked against the Firm's stated assumptions.
---

A **CEO Digest** is the weekly brief each [Firm](firm.md)'s Owner receives: open matters per line, work in progress and
outstanding balances, deadlines, and needs-you items, plus the minutes the Firm's plan work took. Every figure comes
from one journaled query keyed to that Firm, and the model writing the prose receives only those numbers — it writes
around what the queries returned, never instead of them. The brief's assumptions-check block compares each actual
against its [Firm Assumption](firm-assumption.md) and reports one of three verdicts: the assumption is holding, change
the assumption, or change the work. A weekday companion, the [Daily Alert](daily-alert.md), posts only when something
needs the Owner.

## Sections

The digest ships the sections it can source: numbers, needs-you, matter by matter, deadlines, and plan. Sections whose
data does not exist yet — funnel and referrals — arrive with their data rather than shipping as empty blocks.
