---
title: Fee Structure
description: >-
  How an attorney's hour or a matter is priced — effective-dated hourly rates
  and a matter's current flat fee.
---

A **Fee Structure** is how the firm prices work: an attorney's hourly rate, or a matter's flat fee. An
[Attorney Rate](attorney-rate.md) prices one [attorney's](person.md) hour either firm-wide or as a per-matter override;
rate rows are effective-dated and never edited, so the table is its own history, and [Time Entries](time-entry.md)
carry the rate they were captured under, immune to every later edit. A matter's flat fee is the currently agreed
figure, not history. Contingency structures are not part of this model. Amounts are integer cents, never floats.

- Vocabulary: [`store::attorney_rates`](../../store/src/attorney_rates.rs)
