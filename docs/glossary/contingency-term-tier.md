---
title: "Contingency Term Tier"
description: "One stage-based percentage within a Contingency Term."
---

A tier belongs to one [Contingency Term](contingency-term.md), names one [Case Stage](case-stage.md), and records its
order and fee in basis points. The stage belongs to the matter's Firm.

The table is `contingency_term_tier`.

Schema: [`contingency_term_tier`](../../store/postgres/schema.sql).
