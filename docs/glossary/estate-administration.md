## Estate Administration

Estate Administration is the work of identifying and managing a person's property, addressing obligations, and making
distributions after death under applicable law. The authority of a personal representative and the need for a court
proceeding depend on the facts and jurisdiction.

Vesta may discuss legal guidance or a fiduciary role through a separate [Engagement / Retainer](engagement--retainer.md)
after reviewing the role, applicable law, conflicts, and fees. [Trust Administration](trust-administration.md) concerns
carrying out a trust and may proceed alongside estate administration.
