---
title: "IOLTA Account"
description: >-
  A local mirror of one pooled trust bank account for a jurisdiction.
---

The pooled account holding client funds for matters governed by one [Jurisdiction](jurisdiction.md). Navigator mirrors
the account recorded in Xero; the [Journal Entry](journal-entry.md) records the Firm's authoritative accounting.

The table is `iolta_account`.

Schema: [`iolta_account`](../../store/src/schema/navigator.surql#L218).
