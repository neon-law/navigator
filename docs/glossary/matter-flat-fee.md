---
title: "Matter Flat Fee"
description: "The current agreed flat fee for one Project, recorded in integer cents."
---

One [Project](project.md) has at most one current Matter Flat Fee. The record carries the agreed amount and its
insertion time. Unlike an [Attorney Rate](attorney-rate.md), it records the current figure rather than dated history.

The table is `matter_flat_fee`.

Schema: [`matter_flat_fee`](../../store/postgres/schema.sql).
