---
title: "Covenant"
description: "A Covenant is a legal, professional, or contractual obligation Navigator must honor."
---

A Covenant is an obligation arising from applicable law, a lawyer's professional duties, an engagement agreement, or a
published service promise. Privacy, confidentiality, billing, and cancellation are examples of behavior it constrains.
It is a design constraint, not a database table or a claim that the application satisfies every jurisdiction's law.

Navigator combines compatible requirements and applies the stronger protection when several govern the same behavior. A
conflict between duties requires a lawyer's decision; satisfying one requirement does not silently waive another. Public
promises describe implemented, tested behavior and its limits. A provider integration alone does not establish
compliance or replace a filing, consent, or professional judgment.
