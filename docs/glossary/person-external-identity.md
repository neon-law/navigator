---
title: "Person External Identity"
description: >-
  An external provider identity recorded against one Person.
---

Internal identity plumbing for a [Person](person.md), identified by its external system and immutable identifier. The
recorded association itself grants no session or matter access.

The table is `person_external_identity`.

Schema: [`person_external_identity`](../../store/src/schema/navigator.surql#L744).
