---
title: "Xero Invoice"
description: >-
  A local mirror of one invoice Xero issued for a Project.
---

One invoice for a [Project](project.md), identified by its Xero invoice identifier. Xero holds the receivables ledger;
Navigator uses this mirror to show invoice information without a live provider request.

The table is `xero_invoice`.

Schema: [`xero_invoice`](../../store/src/schema/navigator.surql#L146).
