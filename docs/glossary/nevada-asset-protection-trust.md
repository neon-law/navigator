## Nevada Asset Protection Trust

A Nevada Asset Protection Trust is an irrevocable spendthrift trust considered for a person who may also receive
distributions from it. Nevada law requires a qualified Nevada trustee when the settlor is a beneficiary, and part of the
trust's administration must occur in Nevada. Its effect on creditor claims depends on the transfer, the trust terms, and
the laws that apply to the person and property. It does not promise protection from every claim.

Vesta offers planning through a custom [Engagement / Retainer](engagement--retainer.md). Optional [Trust
Administration](trust-administration.md) and an individual trustee appointment have separate terms. The public scope
lives in [Vesta's services catalog](../../neon/locales/en/vesta/services.yaml).
