## Astrology and Tarot

[Vesta Estate Planning](vesta-estate-planning.md) offers an optional astrology and tarot reading at the planning
meeting, before discussing the person's life and wishes. The person may begin directly with that conversation instead.

Readings are for personal reflection. The person's wishes guide the estate plan, and the reading does not supply legal
advice. This is a service choice in the public copy, with no separate database table or workflow state.
