---
title: "Notation Meter Entry"
description: >-
  One priced quantity of work recorded against a Project.
---

A [Project](project.md)'s quoted or tracked work quantity, optionally linked to a [Notation](notation.md) or [Notation
Package](notation-package.md). Its saved unit price keeps later rate edits from changing that recorded quote.

The table is `notation_meter_entry`.

Schema: [`notation_meter_entry`](../../store/src/schema/navigator.surql#L1795).
