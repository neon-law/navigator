---
title: "Reconciliation"
description: >-
  Reconciliation compares recorded accounting with independently evidenced source movements for a declared scope.
---

Reconciliation records the Firm, period, linked sources, expected and actual quantities, checks, repairs, and
exceptions. A Project statement does not prove complete account or IOLTA reconciliation. Repairs require exact approved
commands; posted accounting changes through balanced corrections. Audit records live in `journal_reconciliation`.
