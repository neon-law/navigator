---
title: Expense Category
description: An Expense Category groups matter expenses by the service or charge they cover.
---

An Expense Category identifies what a matter expense paid for. A [Receipt Document](receipt-document.md) records it as
one of `court_filing_fee`, `e_filing_service_fee`, `deposition_and_court_reporter`, `transcript`,
`certified_mail_and_postage`, `process_service`, or `other`.
