---
title: "Client Submissions"
description: "A Project's separate Google Drive intake folder, writable by its participating clients."
---

Client Submissions is a Google Drive intake root outside a [Project](project.md)'s firm working tree. Participating
clients receive Writer access there. Participating Lawyers retain access to both roots. Clerks retain their supervised
Navigator lens without a Drive grant. Google permissions and Navigator [participation](participation.md) remain separate
checks.

A [Project Folder](project-folder.md) binding retains its provider identity. Reconciliation establishes safe intake
before removing known client grants from working material, and verifies the complete provider tree before publishing
folder resources. Unknown grants or unsupported isolation capabilities block publication.

Recursive import copies admitted files into [Client Production](matter-folder.md), preserving nested checkout paths and
provider provenance while retaining stable [Document Identity](document-identity.md). Document visibility continues to
follow the canonical ingest and access rules.

The Project resource panel offers this link only through the Lawyer matter lens. Client and Clerk views receive neither
this link nor the private working-root link. Portal upload remains a separate gated intake door.
