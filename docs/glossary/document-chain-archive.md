---
title: "Document Chain Archive"
description: "The retained archive of one soft-deleted Document chain on a Project."
---

A Document Chain Archive tracks one chain and deletion time through archiving, restoration, or purge. Its
[items](document-chain-archive-item.md) record the verified copies of individual revisions. It belongs to one
[Project](project.md) and its Firm.

The table is `document_chain_archive`.

Schema: [`document_chain_archive`](../../store/postgres/schema.sql).
