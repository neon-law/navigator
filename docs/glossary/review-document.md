---
title: "Review Document"
description: >-
  One attorney-reviewed instrument presented for client review before signing.
---

A [Notation](notation.md)'s rendered instrument for [Client Review](client-review.md), carrying its title, content, and
review status. A matter can have several instruments, each with its own comments.

The table is `review_document`.

Schema: [`review_document`](../../store/src/schema/navigator.surql#L2004).
