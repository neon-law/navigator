---
title: "Document Chain Archive Item"
description: "One Document revision's verified copy within a Document Chain Archive."
---

Each item names its [Document Chain Archive](document-chain-archive.md), Asset, storage and archive object keys, content
digest, size, and verification time. The archive and Asset together identify the item.

The table is `document_chain_archive_item`.

Schema: [`document_chain_archive_item`](../../store/postgres/schema.sql).
