---
title: "Expunge Record"
description: >-
  The audit record of an authorized removal of material from a matter repository.
---

Evidence of who authorized a governed removal, its category, and repository heads before and after it. It serves [Soft
Deletion](soft-deletion.md) and governed expunge while keeping the removed content out of the audit row.

The table is `expunge_record`.

Schema: [`expunge_record`](../../store/src/schema/navigator.surql#L2224).
