---
title: "IOLTA Allocation"
description: >-
  One matter's share of a pooled trust withdrawal, applied to one invoice.
---

The part of an [IOLTA Withdrawal](iolta-withdrawal.md) applied to one [Xero Invoice](xero-invoice.md). Its Project comes
from that invoice, so the matter sees its own share of the transfer.

The table is `iolta_allocation`.

Schema: [`iolta_allocation`](../../store/src/schema/navigator.surql#L268).
