---
title: "Sent Email"
description: >-
  The delivery record of one outbound email attempt.
---

The recipient, content, time, and provider outcome recorded by [Email Send](email-send.md). A known Project links the
delivery to that matter; firm mail may have no Project.

The table is `sent_email`.
