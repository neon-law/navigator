---
title: "Blog Video"
description: >-
  One Brand's stored video available for a Post.
---

A video object owned by a [Brand](brand.md), with its dimensions and orientation. A [Post](post.md) can reference the
object when presenting its video.

The table is `blog_video`.

Schema: [`blog_video`](../../store/src/schema/navigator.surql#L1039).
