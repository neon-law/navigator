---
title: "Lead Consent"
description: >-
  An append-only record of the SMS and callback choices supplied with a contact request.
---

Evidence of the SMS wording, policy version, and opt-in choice, the callback wording and opt-in choice, the time, and
the source page presented when a [Lead](lead.md) submitted a request. A choice counts only when the submission carries a
phone number to reach.

The table is `lead_consent`.

Schema: [`lead_consent`](../../store/postgres/schema.sql).
