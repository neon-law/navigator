## Trust Administration

Trust Administration is the work of carrying out a trust under its terms and applicable law, including records,
communications, accountings, and distributions. It can arise after a death, during incapacity, or throughout an ongoing
trust.

Vesta offers legal guidance through a custom [Engagement / Retainer](engagement--retainer.md). An individual trustee
appointment requires separate acceptance and written terms. A trustee's [Person–Entity Role](personentity-role.md)
records a relationship; it does not create an appointment. The work belongs to a [Matter](matter.md), with no separate
administration table or automated appointment flow.

The public scope lives in [Vesta's services catalog](../../neon/locales/en/vesta/services.yaml). See the [marketing-copy
guidance](../marketing-copy.md#vesta-administration) for its source basis.
