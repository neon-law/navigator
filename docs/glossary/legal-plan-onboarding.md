**Legal Plan Onboarding** is the [Onboarding](onboarding.md) letter for an individual referred by a [Legal
Plan](legal-plan.md). The letter describes the work, the plan benefit, which fees the plan covers, what the member pays,
and how the Firm handles costs.

A newly accepted ARAG or LegalEASE referral opens a [Notation](notation.md) from a shared [Template](template.md) that
pins the corresponding Legal Plan, technology, and arbitration [Clauses](clause.md). The lawyer reviews the plan
authorization and the [Plan Fee Schedule](plan-fee-schedule.md) before approving the letter. A signed [Scope
Amendment](scope-amendment.md) records any later expansion of the agreed work.
