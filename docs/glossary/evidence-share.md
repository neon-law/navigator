---
title: "Evidence Share"
description: >-
  An Evidence Share lets a named recipient accept access terms, then view and download selected filed revisions.
---

A lawyer on the matter creates an Evidence Share from a filed WhatsApp text export. The invitation expires after 14 days
and can be revoked. The recipient verifies the invited email address by signing in and electronically accepts the
recorded terms before viewing or downloading the export and the image revisions selected at invitation creation.
The share grants no Project participation.

The invitation records the acceptance and counts views and download requests. Daily usage totals omit recipient and
matter identifiers.
