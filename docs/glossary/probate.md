## Probate

Probate is the legal process for handling an estate after death, including addressing debts and transferring property.
Whether court proceedings are needed depends on the property and applicable law.

Probate may form part of [Estate Administration](estate-administration.md). It is distinct from [Trust
Administration](trust-administration.md).

See the [Nevada courts' probate overview](https://selfhelp.nvcourts.gov/pro/overview).
