---
title: "Evidence Index"
description: >-
  The evidence index matter section: a matter's exhibits grouped by the claim each bears on, with dates and provenance.
---

The `evidence_index` [Section](section.md) on a [Matter Page](matter-page.md): the matter's exhibits, grouped by the
claim each bears on, with the date each exhibit itself bears and where it came from (LAW-185).

An exhibit appears under every claim its [Evidence](evidence.md) names. Exhibits whose evidence names no claim read
under a "no claim recorded" rail; exhibits filed with no evidence block at all count in the section's note, because the
point of the index is what is not yet tied down. Within a claim, exhibits sort by the date they bear, undated last. Firm
working state: the lawyer lens only, never a client page.

Section name `evidence_index`, optional on the Matter Status Console and Docket Deadline Board kinds.
