---
title: "Pitch"
description: >-
  Pitch is an open Project whose onboarding DocuSign envelope UUID has not been verified as completed.
---

An open [Project](project.md) remains a pitch until Navigator holds its [Onboarding](onboarding.md) letter's DocuSign
envelope UUID and verifies completion in the owning Firm's account. An outstanding, declined, voided, or absent envelope
leaves the Project a pitch.

The execution rule is defined by [Official](official.md). The [Litigation Pitch](litigation-pitch.md) is a Matter Page.
