---
title: "Pitch"
description: >-
  Pitch is an open Project whose onboarding execution has yet to be verified.
---

An open [Project](project.md) remains a pitch until Navigator holds verified execution of its
[Onboarding](onboarding.md) letter. An outstanding, declined, voided, or absent signature request leaves it a pitch.

The execution rule is defined by [Official](official.md). The [Litigation Pitch](litigation-pitch.md) is a Matter Page.
