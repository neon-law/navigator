---
title: "Pitch"
description: >-
  Pitch is the onboarding reading of a matter whose onboarding document has not completed a signature loop Navigator
  sent.
---

The [Onboarding](onboarding.md) reading of a matter that is not yet [Official](official.md): its onboarding notation has
no completed envelope on a signature loop [Navigator](matter.md) itself sent. Nothing signed yet, an envelope still
outstanding, one declined, or a completion Navigator did not send — all read pitch. It is the `pitch` value of the
closed enum [`OnboardingStatus`](../../store/src/projects.rs).

Pitch here is the execution reading of one matter's onboarding, not the [Litigation Pitch](litigation-pitch.md) matter
page and not the `pitch` value of the lifecycle, which a matter keeps until work starts.
