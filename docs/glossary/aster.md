## Aster

Aster the Astrocartographer is [Vesta Estate Planning](vesta-estate-planning.md)’s illustrated star companion: a golden
star with a teal scarf, a satchel, and a paintbrush as tall as a walking staff. Aster introduces the practice’s optional
reflection on places, relationships, and possibilities through [Astrocartography](astrocartography.md).

The character is a public illustration, not a Person, legal adviser, or calculated astrology chart. Website poses live
as editable SVGs in `server/public/brand/aster/`, with named groups for the body, face, scarf, satchel, and brush.
