---
title: "Inbox Delivery Attempt"
description: >-
  One outbound transmission attempt or provider callback for a Message.
---

Delivery evidence for a [Message](message.md), including its provider result. An unknown outcome remains recorded for
reconciliation before another transmission is considered.

The table is `inbox_delivery_attempt`.
