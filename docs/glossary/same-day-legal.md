A house [Brand](brand.md) of Lex Tecnica, Ltd., keyed `same-day-legal` in
[`views::brand::BrandKey`](../../views/src/brand.rs) and served at `sameday.neonlaw.com` (staging:
`staging-sameday.neonlaw.com`). It wears the Lex Tecnica brand kit: monochrome black, white, and light grey, Cormorant
SC display type, and Nunito Sans as the self-hosted stand-in for Avenir.

For $29/mo or $290/year the subscription runs a set of checks, each an equal peer: data removal, privacy protection,
credit monitoring, dark web monitoring, identity theft insurance, unclaimed property searches and claims, basic form
filings such as forming an LLC, and attorney review of the client's own work. It also includes limited changes to an
estate plan the client makes with [Vesta Estate Planning](vesta-estate-planning.md); the plan itself is a separate
engagement. Government fees for a filing are separate. Monitoring and insurance coverage are confirmed before
activation. The subscription includes $5 a month of model usage in the [Legal Sandbox](legal-sandbox.md), and the home
previews the coming iPhone and Android apps.

A client chooses one of two ways to pay: the subscription, or a single attorney review for $50 paid once. A client who
wants neither can prepare their own filings with Navigator, the free and open-source MCP tool, with no lawyer review.

The subscription bills through the same Purchase offerings as [DeleteYourData.com](brand.md)
(`delete-your-data-monthly`, `delete-your-data-annual`). Neon's `/same-day-legal` link, and the retired `/nautilus`
page, answer a 301 to the Same Day Legal host in the current deployment.

An active Same Day Legal subscription is what lets an optional [Death Check](death-check.md) set the triggers in a
[Vesta Estate Planning](vesta-estate-planning.md) estate plan in motion on the client's behalf.
