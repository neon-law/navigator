---
title: "Beneficial Ownership"
description: "Beneficial Ownership records whether a journal quantity belongs to the Firm or to a client."
---

Beneficial Ownership is independent of the account holding a quantity and its asset type. A Firm-owned journal can
record client-owned funds without turning them into earned fees. [Journal Postings](journal-posting.md) retain ownership
and restrictions alongside their exact quantities. Statements keep distinct ownership and restrictions separate.

Historical unspecified ownership remains explicit and requires review. New posting commands declare Firm or client
ownership. A noncash or crypto receipt does not establish eligibility for IOLTA custody.
