G&S is shorthand for Guieb & Shook PC, the seeded [Firm](firm.md) of the Neon Law deployment. "A G&S matter" is a
[Project](project.md) that Guieb & Shook PC owns, whatever [Brand](brand.md) it wears.

G&S is the Firm with Google Drive and Google Calendar turned on in its [Firm Integrations](firm-integrations.md), so its
Projects get a private Drive folder and a shared [Project Calendar](project-calendar.md). A Firm with those turned off
records dates as Navigator [Deadlines](deadline.md) and hands out ICS files instead of a shared calendar.

Use the full name, Guieb & Shook PC, in client-facing copy and letters. G&S is for internal notes, Linear issues, and
agent instructions.
