Another name for the [DeleteYourData.com](brand.md) subscription: non-legal data removal and privacy protection, plus
unclaimed property searches of government databases with claims processed on the client's behalf, and basic form filings
such as forming an LLC, for $29/mo or $290/year prepaid. Government fees for a filing are separate. Nautilus is a
non-legal service: legal fees are excluded from both plans and must be prepaid. Gift cards are available. It is
**client-English, not a schema noun**: a client may know the product by either name, and both name the same
subscription, not a separate [Brand](brand.md) or host. Neon Law describes it at `/nautilus`.

An active Neon Law Nautilus subscription is what lets an optional [Death Check](death-check.md) set the triggers in a
[Vesta Estate Planning](vesta-estate-planning.md) estate plan in motion on the client's behalf.
