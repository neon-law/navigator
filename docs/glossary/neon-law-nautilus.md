Another name for the [DeleteYourData.com](brand.md) subscription, published on Neon's `/nautilus` page as plain
**Nautilus**. For $29/mo or $290/year prepaid it runs a set of checks, each an equal peer: data removal, privacy
protection, credit monitoring, dark web monitoring, identity theft insurance, unclaimed property searches and claims, an
estate planning check-in, and basic form filings such as forming an LLC. Government fees for a filing are separate.
Monitoring and insurance coverage are confirmed before activation. Gift cards are available.

Nautilus is a non-legal service: legal fees are excluded from both plans and must be prepaid. A lawyer review of the
client's own work starts at $20 under its own engagement. The subscription includes $5 a month of model usage in the
[Legal Sandbox](legal-sandbox.md). The page previews the coming iPhone and Android apps.

It is **client-English, not a schema noun**: a client may know the product by either name, and both name the same
subscription, not a separate [Brand](brand.md) or host.

An active Neon Law Nautilus subscription is what lets an optional [Death Check](death-check.md) set the triggers in a
[Vesta Estate Planning](vesta-estate-planning.md) estate plan in motion on the client's behalf.
