---
title: "DocuSign completion"
description: "A DocuSign completion tracks filing for one completed envelope in a Firm's DocuSign account."
---

A verified Connect delivery and a scheduled account poll register the same completion by Firm, account, and envelope.
The worker records the Project assignment before filing the signed PDF and completion certificate. Unresolved
assignments go to the Firm for review.

The table is `docusign_completion`.
