---
title: "Lead Intake"
description: "The answers a visitor submits with a contact request."
---

A Lead Intake records the answers submitted with a [Lead](lead.md). Each submission belongs to the Firm that received
it. Guieb & Shook records the visitor's name and inquiry. IEP Defenders records the school or district, the visitor's
relationship to the student, the concern, and any upcoming date.

The table is `lead_intake`.
