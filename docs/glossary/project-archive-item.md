---
title: "Project Archive Item"
description: "One verified item within a Project Recovery Archive generation."
---

An item belongs to one [Project Recovery Archive](project-recovery-archive.md) generation and records its manifest
identity, content digest, size, and verified stored copy. The generation commits after its expected items verify.

The table is `project_archive_item`.

Schema: [`project_archive_item`](../../store/postgres/schema.sql).
