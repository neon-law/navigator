Privacy Policy

Effective November 27, 2026

This policy explains what information the firm collects through this website and with whom it may be shared. It covers this website only; information about clients and their matters is governed by the firm’s professional obligations of confidentiality.

Information you send us

The site’s contact form does not send or store anything itself: it opens your own email app with your note filled in, and nothing reaches the firm unless you send that email. If you email or call the firm, it receives what you choose to send, such as your name, contact details and a description of your matter.

Information collected automatically

The server that hosts this site keeps limited log data, such as your IP address.

Client sign-in

Clients of the firm can use Sign in to reach their matters in the firm’s client portal. When you sign in, the firm receives your email address and a record that you signed in. If you choose to sign in with Google, Microsoft or Apple, that company handles the sign-in under its own privacy policy and tells the firm who you are. What the portal holds about you and your matters is kept confidential under the firm’s professional obligations.

Cookies and analytics

The website’s pages do not set cookies. The sign-in page and the client portal use only the cookies needed to keep signing in secure and to keep you signed in; they are not used for tracking or advertising.

To understand how the site is used, the firm uses Plausible Analytics, a privacy-focused analytics service that does not use cookies. Plausible records the pages you visit, the site or link that brought you here, your browser, operating system and device type, your approximate location (country, region or city), and whether you click a phone number, a link to another site or the contact form’s Send button. It does not store your IP address, which is used only to count unique visits. The firm cannot use this information to identify you and does not use it for advertising.

The site has no advertising trackers, does not use session replay and does not record your keystrokes. No third party collects information about your activity over time or across other websites through this site.

Fonts

This website’s fonts are hosted with its own files rather than loaded from Google’s font servers, so viewing a page does not send your IP address to Google.

Sharing

The firm shares information only with service providers that help it run the website and its practice, under duties of confidentiality, or when the law or the rules of professional conduct require it. The firm does not sell personal information and does not share it for advertising.

Changes and contact

The firm may update this policy and will post the new version here with its date. Questions about this policy can be sent to info@guiebshook.com.
