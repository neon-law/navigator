Overview

Nicholas Shook is a business litigator whose practice also covers privacy, technology and estate planning. He represents businesses across the full range of commercial disputes, including disputes involving cryptocurrency, and has argued appeals and handled hearings. He has also been involved in more than $100 million in mergers and acquisitions.

During five years as a software engineer at Apple, he helped Apple’s finance organization comply with the GDPR, China’s Personal Information Protection Law and the California Consumer Privacy Act, and migrated 20 petabytes of financial data to the cloud. Earlier, he built an accounts receivable system used by a national bank.

That engineering background lets him work fluently with technical teams and spot privacy risks early, and it shapes the systems the firm uses to run its matters.

Representative experience

Before founding Guieb & Shook, Nicholas:

  • Won an appeal in Nevada in a civil rights action under 42 U.S.C. § 1983 alleging Eighth Amendment violations arising from the abuse of an incarcerated person.
  • Obtained cannabis business licenses for clients in Nevada and Illinois.
  • Litigated business disputes involving cryptocurrency.
