Business Litigation

Litigating complex commercial and securities disputes for companies, shareholders and investors in state and federal court.

When a business dispute has to be litigated, we represent companies, their owners and their investors in state and federal court, including in complex cases with multiple parties, related proceedings in other courts and expert valuation of securities. We handle each case from the first pleading through discovery, motions and settlement or trial.

Our work spans commercial disputes over contracts, partnerships and business torts, and securities disputes between companies and the people who invest in them: fraud or misrepresentation in the sale of securities, financing disputes, and shareholder claims.

Many securities disputes turn on information: what a company disclosed, what an investor was told, or what others publish about a company and its leadership. When false statements about a company’s stock are spread to move the price, the matter can involve defamation and market manipulation as well as securities claims.

What we handle

  • Commercial disputes, including breach of contract and business torts
  • Shareholder and partnership disputes, including claims by and against shareholders
  • Securities fraud and misrepresentation in the sale of securities
  • Financing disputes with investment funds and institutional lenders
  • False statements about a company’s stock, including defamation by online publishers
  • Market manipulation, including short-and-distort schemes
  • Injunctions and other emergency relief

Who we represent

Public and private companies and their boards and officers, business owners, shareholders and investors.
