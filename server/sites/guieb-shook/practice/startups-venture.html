Startups & Venture

Entity structuring, equity, fundraising and investor documents.

Early decisions about structure and equity are hard to undo later. We help founders get them right from the start, in plain terms and at a pace that fits a company that is still finding its footing.

When it is time to raise money, we prepare and negotiate the documents, keep the capitalization table accurate and explain what each term means for control and ownership.

What we handle

  • Formation and founder agreements
  • Equity, vesting and option plans
  • SAFEs, convertible notes and priced rounds
  • Investor documents and capitalization tables
  • Intellectual property assignment and contractor agreements
  • Advisor agreements

Who we represent

Founders and early-stage companies.
