Dispute Resolution

Resolving conflicts efficiently, in negotiation or in court.

Most disputes are best resolved early and on clear terms. We start by understanding what you need from the outcome, then choose the path most likely to get you there: a demand letter, a negotiated settlement, mediation or arbitration, or litigation in state or federal court.

When a case goes to court, we handle it from the first pleading through discovery, motions and trial preparation. You will know where the matter stands, what comes next and what it is likely to cost.

What we handle

  • Breach of contract and other commercial disputes
  • Disputes among business owners, partners and shareholders
  • Fraud and misrepresentation claims
  • Pre-suit demands and settlement negotiations
  • Mediation and arbitration
  • Enforcing and collecting judgments

Who we represent

Individuals, founders and businesses, as plaintiffs or defendants.
