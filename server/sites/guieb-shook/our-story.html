Our story

Built in New York, across three boroughs.

The firm took shape over a season of conversations that moved across New York City, from Manhattan to Brooklyn and Staten Island.

Manhattan

Where a legal engineers meetup sparked the first conversations about the firm, and where we kept building the systems behind it.

Brooklyn

Where the practice found its rhythm: long working sessions, conversations with fellow practitioners, live matters on the table, and the systems to support them taking shape alongside.

Staten Island

Where hikes on La Tourette Trail and working sessions over coffee in Historic Richmond Town shaped the firm’s direction.

Our name

Guieb

Guieb is the family name of Waleed Amer’s mother. Her father, Teodulo Florendo Guieb, was an attorney in Manila, Philippines.

The firm carries his name to honor him and her, and the tradition of counsel they passed down.

&

Shook

Nicholas Shook is the proud son of Richard and Me Ray, who met serendipitously despite growing up half a world apart.

The firm carries the Shook name in that same spirit: bridging the distance between people, places and ideas.
